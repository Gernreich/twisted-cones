Spiral Ellipse Sweep

← All projects · Twisted cones

The same ellipse at the same tilt, moved out to run from (20, 0, 0) to (10, 0, 190), y within ±10, centre (15, 0, 95). As it rises by h it turns counter-clockwise about the z axis (seen from above) by τ·h/1000, shrinks about its own centre from 1 to 0.1, and its centre closes in on the z axis from 15 to 1. Every position is kept, so the sweep shows as a lit solid.

The solid is every position of the ellipse from h = 0 to 1000, closed by the first and last ellipse. The heavy ring is the ellipse at the slider and shows through the solid. The amber line is the path of the centre, a spiral that tightens from radius 15 to radius 1, carried on past the sweep both ways: down to z = 0 at (13.51, −9.18, 0), and up until it reaches the z axis at (0, 0, 1166.43), after 1.071 turns. Under the geometric law the radius never reaches 0, so the line stops at h = 1000 there. No point of the solid comes closer than 0.5 to the z axis. x and y start stretched ×4; the true shape is about 40 wide and 1105 tall.
