Spiral Circle Sweep

← All projects · Twisted cones

A flat circle, always level with the x/y plane, centred on the amber centre line of the Spiral Ellipse Sweep. Its radius is the centre's distance from the z axis, so its edge always touches x = 0, y = 0. It turns counter-clockwise about the z axis by τ per 1000 of rise and lifts from z = 0 until the circle shrinks to nothing on the axis.
